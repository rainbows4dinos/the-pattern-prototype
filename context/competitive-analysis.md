- Broader ecosystem map spanning direct competitors (Co-Star, Moonly, Chani,

- Reference apps mapped to the specific design problems they solve for The

## Key findings

- Conversational AI depth is the clearest white space in the astrology app
  category. Nobody owns "dedicated and deep."
- The "dedicated + deep" quadrant is the most contested.
- Vedic vs. Western is a meaningful third competitive axis, beyond the usual
  casual-vs-serious framing.
