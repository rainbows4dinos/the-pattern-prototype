# Design principles

Established across the engagement. These survive brand changes. They are about
interaction and structure, not visual style.

## Interactive centerpiece over passive scroll

One interactive focal object per screen. The screen is built around a single
object the user manipulates, not a passive scroll of cards or a stack to swipe
through. Card stacks are a known anti-pattern for Larry.

Worked examples:
- Lens: an interactive date/moon/planet cycle slider instead of a card stack.
- You: a personality wheel as the focal object.

## Synthesis over transcription

Whether auditing or presenting, abstract to patterns. Do not transcribe
individual findings or annotations. State the root cause, not the list of
symptoms.

## Keep attention on In-Depth

In-Depth is one of The Pattern's most compelling features and also the
functionally messiest. It rewards design attention out of proportion to its
screen count.

## Build on Larry's mid-session concepts

Larry introduces ideas during the work (the cycle slider is one). Treat these as
seeds to develop, not requests to fulfill literally.
