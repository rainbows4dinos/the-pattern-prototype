How you act

Your Mars in Pisces

Your Mars in Pisces doesn't push. It moves like water around whatever's in the way, which reads as gentleness right up until you notice the water got where it was going. You act on feeling and instinct more than plan, and confrontation costs you more than it costs most people.

That's the baseline. Your Mars doesn't sit alone, though. Two slow planets lean on it. Scroll, or tap one.

Saturn square Mars · a complex

Saturn makes drive itself feel risky. It asks what your wanting will cost before you've moved a step. The useful version is discipline. The stuck version is a brake you forgot you were holding. In Pisces it rarely arrives as a clean no. It's fog with a weight in it.

Neptune square Mars · a complex

Neptune dissolves the target. Desire arrives already idealized, so you chase the idea of the thing instead of the thing. The work isn't to want less. It's to check, now and then, whether it's actually there.

Between them, the pattern: you either don't move, or you move toward a mirage. Catching which, in the moment, is most of the work.

Your Mars beyond you
Cosmic climate · right now

For the next week, transiting Mars is crossing your own. It brings the fog forward and makes wanting feel slippery. If your drive's been hard to find lately, that's weather, not a permanent condition.

Pattern recognition

About one person in twelve carries Mars in Pisces. A few are almost certainly in your contacts. Recognizing it in someone else is usually the fastest way to understand it in yourself.

A Bond

This kind of pressure shows up between two people as clearly as inside one. If the hesitation feels familiar from someone specific, that isn't a coincidence.
