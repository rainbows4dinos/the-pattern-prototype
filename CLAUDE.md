## Who Larry is

larry.jr.design@gmail.com. Fractional Head of Design at The Pattern, a consumer
astrology and psychological self-discovery app. Engaged since late June 2026.
Principal-level product designer. Systems thinking is the through-line: design
systems, AI-native workflows, and moving between strategy and functional
prototyping. Fractional is a deliberate choice. The mandate is high-leverage
design leadership, not staff-level execution. A colleague, Allison, also
contributes to the audit.

## How he works

- Fast. Delegates decisively. Wants strong strategic calls and proactively
  surfaced constraints, not multiple-choice menus.
- Wants direct pushback when something is off. Name the real constraint rather
  than executing the surface request.
- Iterates at the word level. One clean version at a time, not several variants.
- Short directional instructions. Trusts reasonable design decisions without
  heavy specification.
- Feedback is direct, sometimes shorthand (e.g. "Still white, mister Fable" to
  flag an unresolved bug).
- Verifies and manually transfers critical outputs when automation is uncertain.
- Brings design concepts mid-session and wants a collaborative exchange, not just
  output. Build on what he introduces.

## Writing and tone

Warm but grounded. Brief. No flattery or self-congratulation. No em dashes. No AI
hype language, no startup clichés, no piling on superlatives. Dry and deadpan
works for public-facing copy.

## Design direction

- Interactive centerpiece over passive scroll. One interactive focal object per
  screen (cycle-slider for Lens, personality wheel for You). Card stacks are a
  known anti-pattern for him.
- Synthesis over transcription.
- In-Depth is one of The Pattern's most compelling but functionally messiest
  features. Keep design attention on it.
- Visual direction and brand are unsettled as of July 2026. Early prototypes are
  intentionally wireframe-level and brand-neutral. Do not lock a palette or
  typographic voice yet. See design-system/tokens.md.
